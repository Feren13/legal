Account Deletion

Updated: July 25, 2026

1. How to delete your account in the app

Open Ekhora → Profile → Account Settings → the "Delete Account" button at the bottom of the screen. Once confirmed, your account and the data listed in section 2 are deleted immediately and cannot be recovered.

2. What gets deleted

  • Profile: name, age, gender, city, photo references, your "about me" text, tags/interests, personality test results and traits.
  • Game data: earned and purchased archetype cards, gems, stones, purchased cosmetics.
  • Activity: your swipes ("interested"/"skip") and block lists in both directions.
  • Communities: your community memberships and posts you published.
  • Messaging: messages you sent, and the chats you took part in.
  • Account: your sign-in credentials (email/password) in Firebase Authentication.

3. What is not deleted automatically

  • Media files (photos, audio) you uploaded are stored with a third-party provider (Cloudinary) and are not automatically removed from there together with your profile — you can request their deletion separately by email.
  • Communities where you are listed as the owner are not deleted or transferred automatically — before deleting your account, transfer ownership to another member or contact us to resolve the community's fate.
  • Certain records of interaction with other users' content (e.g. praises/stones you gave, notifications) are technically linked to your identifier and are not cascade-deleted with your profile — you can request their deletion separately by email.

4. Processing time

Data listed in section 2 is deleted immediately once you confirm in the app — usually within a few seconds. Requests sent by email (including for section 3) are handled manually within 30 days.

5. Deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, or want the data described in section 3 deleted, email us at ekhora.app@gmail.com from the address registered to your Ekhora account — this lets us confirm the request is yours.
