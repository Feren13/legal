Privacy Policy

Updated: July 25, 2026

1. General Provisions

This Privacy Policy describes how the Ekhora application (the "App") collects, uses, and protects your personal data. The data controller is Maksym Shtohryn. By using the App, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old and agree to the terms of this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. What Data We Process

  • Registration and profile: email address, name, age, gender, pronouns.
  • Profile content: photos and videos, interests (tags), "about me" description text; city and country — chosen by you manually from a directory; the App does not request or use device geolocation (GPS).
  • Personality profile: your answers to the personality test and the results computed from them (personality traits and facets). This data is used exclusively to find matches and to display your analytics to you. The test is a personality questionnaire for self-reflection and is not a medical or psychological diagnosis.
  • Communication and activity: messages in private chats (text, photos, videos, voice messages), "interested" and "skip" actions, participation in communities, posts and reports.
  • Technical data: device information, activity logs, identifiers, a push token for delivering notifications, last login time, device/interface language — required for the service's operation and security.

3. Purposes of Processing

We process data to: register you and provide access; find matches and recommendations; show your profile to other users; enable messaging based on mutual interest; maintain safety and moderation (handling reports and blocks); provide support; improve the service.

4. Legal Basis

The legal basis for processing is the consent you give when registering in the App. You may withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account.

5. Who Receives Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. To operate the service, we engage service providers who process data on our behalf: Google Firebase (authentication, database, server functions, hosting); Cloudinary (photo storage and delivery); Expo (push notification delivery infrastructure); and, once purchases are enabled in the future, the Google Play payment system. These providers process data under their own terms and with their own security measures. We may disclose data if required by law.

6. International Transfer

Our service providers' servers may be located outside your country. Data transfers are carried out using the security measures adopted by these providers. We have data processing agreements (DPAs) in place with our providers, including standard contractual clauses where applicable.

7. Storage and Security

We store your data for as long as your account is active. Data transmission is protected by connection encryption (HTTPS). We take reasonable measures to protect information, but absolute security on the Internet cannot be guaranteed. Media files (photos, videos, voice messages) are delivered through the provider's content delivery network (CDN) and are technically accessible via a direct link to anyone who has it; such links are shown only to users of the App in the places provided by the interface.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to your data, its correction or deletion, to receive it in a portable format (data portability), and to withdraw your consent to processing. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights. To exercise these rights, write to us at ekhora.app@gmail.com.

9. Account Deletion

You can delete your account within the App itself or by sending a request to ekhora.app@gmail.com. When your account is deleted, your profile, photo references, test results, messages, and related data are removed immediately. The media files themselves, held by a third-party provider (Cloudinary), are not deleted automatically — you can request their deletion at ekhora.app@gmail.com; such requests are processed within 30 days. Certain anonymized records or information we are legally required to retain may be preserved.

10. Age Restriction

The App is intended only for persons aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we become aware that data was provided by a person under 18, we will delete such data.

11. Analytics

As of publication, we do not use third-party analytics tools for profiling. If we introduce them in the future, we will update this Policy and, where necessary, request your consent.

12. Changes to the Policy

We may update this Policy. The current version is always available in the App with its effective date indicated. We will notify you of material changes through available means.

13. Contacts

For any questions about personal data processing, contact: ekhora.app@gmail.com. Operator: Maksym Shtohryn.
